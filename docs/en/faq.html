Common questions

A place for questions, bug reports, and ideas for Annotateshot on Mac.

Get Annotateshot
Why can’t I capture my screen?

Check Annotateshot’s screen recording permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Quit and relaunch the app after changing permission. The exact setting name varies by macOS version.

What should I include in a bug report?

Include the app version and build number, macOS version, steps to reproduce, expected behavior, and what actually happened. A redacted screenshot or short recording can help.

Where do I request a refund?

App Store payments and refunds are handled by Apple. Use the refund link below. Do not post receipts in public issues.

Does a feature request promise a release date?

No. Requests help us understand what people need. A “planned” label is not a promise of a release date.
